# Knowledge handover for departing knowledge workers (working title)

**Status:** raw. Captured 2026-10-04, with open questions pending.
**Theme (likely):** Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity
**One-liner:** When a project manager or other knowledge worker leaves, an AI reads their documents, asks them *why* they made past decisions, and turns their answers into a knowledge base. Their successor can then query it, get an onboarding guide, and see the reasoning behind decisions.

## The idea as described
1. **Connect** to the leaver's documents and read their history.
2. **Find past decisions** in those documents and **ask the leaver why** each was made.
3. **Update the knowledge base** with their answers.
4. **Keep the loop going:** as the leaver creates or updates documents, ask follow-up questions.
5. **Finish with an exit/onboarding interview.** The process might start about 30 days before the last day; the exact window is up to us.
6. **The successor** can:
   - ask the knowledge source questions
   - get a generated onboarding guide
   - see why things were done the way they were
   - get a list of suggested questions to ask
7. **Two knowledge scopes:**
   - **Project-specific:** this project's decisions and context
   - **Org-wide:** all projects combined, for more general questions
