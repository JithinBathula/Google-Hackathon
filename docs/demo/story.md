# Demo story: Maya leaves, and the Summit is in March

> New here? Read the first section and "The four secrets". That's the whole demo. The rest is reference.

## The 30-second version
A company runs a customer conference every March. **Maya**, who has organised it for three years, has quit. **Ben**, who has never organised an event, is taking over. Maya's documents say *what* to do, but almost never *why*. Four of those missing "whys" would each cost Ben dearly if he guessed wrong.

Our app reads Maya's Google Drive and Calendar, finds where the documents go quiet, and asks Maya those questions before she leaves. Ben then gets a map of everything she knew, reasons included.

All names, companies and events are fictional. The demo data lives in Maya's Google account (mayakestreltest@gmail.com): a "Summit 2027" folder in Drive with 8 files, plus 14 calendar events. Everything in it follows this file. Today in the story is **2026-10-09**.

## Words you'll see
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Keynote** | The main speech of the day, by a well-known guest speaker. Often the reason guests come. |
| **Breakout talks** | Smaller sessions running alongside each other. The Summit has four. |
| **AV** | Audio-visual: microphones, screens, lights and sound in the room. |
| **Livestream** | Broadcasting the event online for people who can't attend. |
| **Sponsor** | A company that pays for part of the event in return for its logo or branding. |
| **Headcount** | The final number of guests, which the caterer needs to know how much food to make. |
| **Gap** | Our app's word for something the documents should explain but don't. Each gap becomes a question for Maya. |

## The setup
**Kestrel** is a mid-size company in Singapore. Every March it holds a one-day customer conference, the **Kestrel Summit**: about 180 guests, a keynote, four breakout talks, lunch, an evening reception. The next one is **Thursday 2027-03-18**.

**Maya Tan**, Events & Marketing Coordinator, has run the Summit for three years (2024, 2025, 2026). She resigned on 2026-10-05. Last day **2026-11-06**.

**Ben Ong**, an account executive in sales, takes over the Summit from 2026-10-19. He has attended one Summit as a guest and has never organised an event.

## The four secrets (the planted gaps)
Only Maya knows these. The documents give the instruction but never the reason, and without the reason Ben would make an expensive mistake. They are used to check the extraction and to script the demo, so **the documents must never say them**.

| # | What the documents say | What Ben might wrongly do | What only Maya knows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marina was picked even though Orchid scored higher and is cheaper. Only "Marina. Jun agreed." | Switch to Orchid to save SGD 6,500 | Orchid **double-booked** the Summit in 2025 and the team found out ten days before the event; Jun said "never again" in a corridor. |
| 2 | "Do not send invitations until the keynote speaker is confirmed in writing", stated flatly | Send invitations early to get more sign-ups | In 2025 invitations went out first, the keynote **cancelled**, and about **forty guests withdrew**. |
| 3 | "AV and livestream: SGD 14,000, TBC, waiting on Farah" and "sort out the sponsor conversation" | Keep chasing Farah, who will never say yes on her own | Farah will approve the livestream **only if a sponsor covers it**. Maya is mid-conversation with a sponsor who is interested. |
| 4 | Contacts sheet: Mr Lim, "call, don't email" | Email him like anyone else | He **doesn't read email**. In 2026 the catering headcount email sat unread for a week and the deadline was nearly missed. |

## The facts the documents state
What is actually in Maya's Drive and Calendar:
- **Summit 2027 plan (2026-09-20, updated 2026-10-02):** date, Hotel Marina Grand Ballroom, agenda outline, target 180 guests, budget SGD 95k, team: Maya (lead), Jun (sponsor), and "AV company and caterer as in 2026".
- **Venue comparison (2026-08-28):** Marina vs Orchid on price, capacity, location, AV, catering, parking. Orchid wins on total score and price. The plan picks Marina. The sheet's notes column says only "Marina confirmed with Mr Lim 2026-09-04".
- **Budget (updated 2026-10-06):** lines for venue, catering, AV, livestream, speaker fees, printing, gifts, contingency. "AV and livestream: SGD 14,000, **TBC, waiting on Farah**". Farah has had it since 2026-09-29.
- **Task timeline (2026-09-20):** month by month from October to March. Includes the rule "**Do not send invitations until the keynote speaker is confirmed in writing**", stated flatly. Keynote confirmation target: 2026-11-30. Invitations: 2026-12-08. Catering headcount to Mr Lim: 2027-03-04.
- **Contacts (updated 2026-10-01):** Mr Lim at Hotel Marina with the note "**call, don't email**"; the caterer; the AV company; two speakers; Farah; Jun.
- **Meeting notes with Jun, 2026-09-04:** venue decision recorded as "Marina. Jun agreed." and nothing more. Other items: budget to Farah, keynote shortlist.
- **Meeting notes with Jun, 2026-10-02:** Maya's resignation, Ben to take over, Jun wants a "proper handover doc", keynote still unconfirmed, AV budget still with Farah, Maya to "sort out the sponsor conversation before she goes".
- **Handover note for Ben (draft, 2026-10-08):** what the Summit is, the key dates, who's who in one line each, open items (keynote, AV budget, sponsor), and a section "**Things only I know**" with four headings and "TODO" under each: "Venue", "Invitations timing", "Farah and the AV line", "Working with Mr Lim".
- **Calendar (2026-07 to 2026-11, about 12 events):** weekly marketing sync (a few instances), venue walkthrough at Hotel Marina 2026-09-04, venue walkthrough at Hotel Orchid 2026-08-26, budget review with Farah 2026-09-29, call with "sponsor (TBC)" 2026-10-14, handover sessions with Ben 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-28, Maya's last day 2026-11-06.
