# Demo story: Maya leaves, and the Summit is in March

The demo data lives in Maya's Google account (mayakestreltest@gmail.com): a "Summit 2027" folder in Drive with 8 files, plus 14 calendar events. Everything in it follows this file. All names, companies and events are fictional. Today in the story is **2026-10-09**.

## The setup
**Kestrel** is a mid-size company in Singapore. Every March it holds a one-day customer conference, the **Kestrel Summit**: about 180 guests, a keynote, four breakout talks, lunch, an evening reception. The next one is **Thursday 2027-03-18**.

**Maya Tan**, Events & Marketing Coordinator, has run the Summit for three years (2024, 2025, 2026). She resigned on 2026-10-05. Last day **2026-11-06**.

**Ben Ong**, an account executive in sales, takes over the Summit from 2026-10-19. He has attended one Summit as a guest and has never organised an event.

## People (five, no more)
| Name | Role | How they appear in the documents |
|---|---|---|
| Maya Tan | Events & Marketing Coordinator. The leaver. | Author of every document |
| Ben Ong | Successor | Only in the October handover note and calendar |
| Jun Wong | Head of Marketing, Maya's boss, owns the Summit budget | Decides things in conversation; "Jun agreed", "per Jun" |
| Mr Lim (Lim Boon Keng) | Events manager at **Hotel Marina**, the venue | Venue contact |
| Farah Hassan | Finance business partner | Approves budget lines over SGD 5,000 |

Suppliers and speakers are named but are not characters: **Lotus Catering** (caterer), **Brightline AV** (AV and livestream), keynote candidates **Dr Lena Koh** and **Rajesh Iyer**. The sponsor is never named: "sponsor (TBC)".

## Places and things
- **Hotel Marina**: the chosen venue, Grand Ballroom, 200 capacity, SGD 48,000 package.
- **Hotel Orchid**: the other venue considered, 220 capacity, SGD 41,500 package, scored higher on the comparison sheet.
- **Summit 2026** (last March): 172 guests, 4.4/5 feedback. Lessons noted only as "see 2026 debrief" with no debrief document in the Drive.
- Budget 2027: **SGD 95,000** total.

## The facts the documents state
- **Summit 2027 plan (2026-09-20, updated 2026-10-02):** date, Hotel Marina Grand Ballroom, agenda outline, target 180 guests, budget SGD 95k, team: Maya (lead), Jun (sponsor), and "AV company and caterer as in 2026".
- **Venue comparison (2026-08-28):** Marina vs Orchid on price, capacity, location, AV, catering, parking. Orchid wins on total score and price. The plan picks Marina. The sheet's notes column says only "Marina confirmed with Mr Lim 2026-09-04".
- **Budget (updated 2026-10-06):** lines for venue, catering, AV, livestream, speaker fees, printing, gifts, contingency. "AV and livestream: SGD 14,000, **TBC, waiting on Farah**". Farah has had it since 2026-09-29.
- **Task timeline (2026-09-20):** month by month from October to March. Includes the rule "**Do not send invitations until the keynote speaker is confirmed in writing**", stated flatly. Keynote confirmation target: 2026-11-30. Invitations: 2026-12-08. Catering headcount to Mr Lim: 2027-03-04.
- **Contacts (updated 2026-10-01):** Mr Lim at Hotel Marina with the note "**call, don't email**"; the caterer; the AV company; two speakers; Farah; Jun.
- **Meeting notes with Jun, 2026-09-04:** venue decision recorded as "Marina. Jun agreed." and nothing more. Other items: budget to Farah, keynote shortlist.
- **Meeting notes with Jun, 2026-10-02:** Maya's resignation, Ben to take over, Jun wants a "proper handover doc", keynote still unconfirmed, AV budget still with Farah, Maya to "sort out the sponsor conversation before she goes".
- **Handover note for Ben (draft, 2026-10-08):** what the Summit is, the key dates, who's who in one line each, open items (keynote, AV budget, sponsor), and a section "**Things only I know**" with four headings and "TODO" under each: "Venue", "Invitations timing", "Farah and the AV line", "Working with Mr Lim".
- **Calendar (2026-07 to 2026-11, about 12 events):** weekly marketing sync (a few instances), venue walkthrough at Hotel Marina 2026-09-04, venue walkthrough at Hotel Orchid 2026-08-26, budget review with Farah 2026-09-29, call with "sponsor (TBC)" 2026-10-14, handover sessions with Ben 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-28, Maya's last day 2026-11-06.

## The four planted gaps: what the documents must NOT say
Only Maya knows these. They are used to check the extraction and to script the demo.
1. **Why Hotel Marina over Hotel Orchid:** Orchid double-booked the Summit in 2025 and the team found out ten days before the event; Jun said "never again" in a corridor. Documents say only "Marina. Jun agreed."
2. **Why invitations wait for the keynote:** in 2025 invitations went out first, the keynote cancelled, about forty guests withdrew. Documents state the rule, never the reason.
3. **What's going on with the AV and livestream budget:** Farah will approve the livestream only if a sponsor covers it; Maya is mid-conversation with a sponsor who is interested. Documents say only "TBC, waiting on Farah" and "sort out the sponsor conversation".
4. **Why "call, don't email" Mr Lim:** he doesn't read email, and in 2026 the catering headcount deadline was nearly missed because the email sat unread for a week. Documents give the instruction, never the reason.

## Tone
Maya writes clearly and briefly. Sheets are tidy. Meeting notes are bullet points with the odd loose end. The handover note is obviously unfinished.
