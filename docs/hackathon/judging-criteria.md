# Judging criteria

> Sources: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html, FAQs, official T&C · Last verified: 2026-09-26

The **same rubric applies to all six themes**. Judges score every submission in a **pre-finale round** (Oct 19 – Nov 6) to pick the Singapore finalists. The judging panel is **to be announced**.

| Criterion | Weight | What the judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation** | **40%** | **Meaningful use of Gen AI:** does it directly solve the problem or improve the solution? **Technical implementation:** is it functional, sound and well built? **Scalability & sustainability:** could it be adopted widely and developed beyond the prototype? *The T&C adds: robustness, **Google Cloud architectural depth**, and technical execution.* |
| **Problem Alignment & Impact** | **25%** | **Problem alignment:** how well does it address the chosen theme? **Potential impact:** does it deliver clear value to its users, organization, industry or community? *The T&C adds: **measurable** real-world viability.* |
| **Innovation & Creativity** | **25%** | **Originality and differentiation:** does it bring a fresh perspective or approach compared with existing solutions? **Innovative use of Gen AI:** does it go beyond a basic or superficial implementation? |
| **User Experience & Solution Design** | **10%** | **Usability and accessibility:** is it intuitive for its intended users? **Experience and integration:** do the parts, including the Gen AI, work together smoothly? *The T&C adds: a clean UI design.* |
