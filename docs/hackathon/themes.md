# Themes (problem statements)

> Source: https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html · Last verified: 2026-09-26

Each team picks **exactly one** theme and submits one solution under it. The same judging rubric applies to every theme (see [judging-criteria.md](judging-criteria.md)).

## 1. BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences
Banking, financial services, insurance and related industries.
- Suggested focus areas: detecting and mitigating fraud or risk, automating complex processes, better customer interactions, and more personalized, better-informed financial experiences.

## 2. Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences
How businesses understand, engage and serve customers, and how they run more efficiently across physical and digital commerce.
- Suggested focus areas: discovery, personalization, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insights and fraud.

## 3. Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency
Efficiency, quality, reliability, supply and demand, and sustainability across manufacturing and industrial operations.
- **Explicit expectation:** show how AI turns *operational data* into insights, recommendations or actions.

## 4. Media, Content & Digital Experiences
How content is created, discovered, personalized, understood or experienced.
- Scope covers media, entertainment, publishing, education, communication and other digital content ecosystems.

## 5. Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity
How individuals and organizations access knowledge, collaborate, make decisions and get everyday work done.
- **Explicit expectation:** show measurable gains in productivity, efficiency, knowledge access or workflow execution.

## 6. Sustainability & Social Impact
A meaningful environmental or societal challenge.
- Suggested focus areas: resource efficiency, accessibility, resilience, community support, and more informed, sustainable decisions.

## Notes
