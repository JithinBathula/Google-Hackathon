# Official FAQ (condensed)

> Source: https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html · Last verified: 2026-09-26

## Building & submission
- **Is a prototype enough?** Yes, a working prototype is enough. It must work end to end and be deployed on Cloud Run, GCP or Firebase.
- **Must it be on Google Cloud?** Yes. Solutions built primarily on other clouds won't be evaluated.
- **Can we use an existing project?** No. Only fresh projects built during the hackathon timeline count.
- **Can we submit to multiple themes?** No. One team, one solution, one theme.
- **What's the submission format?** A deployed link, a demo video under 3 minutes, a public GitHub repo, and a detailed deck.

## Team composition & eligibility
- **Can we mix students and professionals?** No. If any member is a student, the whole team is disqualified. Members must be 21+, based in JAPAC, and working professionals, entrepreneurs or startups.
- **Can members be in different JAPAC countries?** Yes.
- **Can someone outside JAPAC join?** No.
- **Can I go solo?** No. The team minimum is 2, but you can register solo and find a team later.
- **Can I be on two teams?** No.
- **What if a teammate drops out?** Invite a replacement while registration is open (Oct 11 per the FAQ; **Oct 4 per the T&C**).

## Grand Finale & travel
- **Can all 4 members travel?** No, only 2, and extra members can't come even at their own expense.
- **What does "fully sponsored" cover?** Flights, accommodation, and finale-day meals for up to 2 members. Visas are on us.
- **What if a visa is late?** Finalists get about 4 weeks' notice. Flights are booked in each traveller's name, so there are no last-minute swaps.

## Judging & evaluation
- **Who are the judges?** They'll be announced closer to the finale.
- **Are the criteria the same for every theme?** Yes: 40 / 25 / 25 / 10. See [judging-criteria.md](judging-criteria.md).
- **Is there a scoring round before the finale?** Yes. It produces the shortlist of teams invited to Singapore.

## Program logistics
- **Does it cost anything?** No, it's free at every stage.
- **Is there support if we're stuck?** Yes. There will be online sessions on the program and the problem statements. There's also the Discord (https://discord.gg/x5GRzJbKpa) and support+aibuildercup@hack2skill.com.
