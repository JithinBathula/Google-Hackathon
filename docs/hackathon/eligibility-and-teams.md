# Eligibility & team rules

> Sources: https://aibuildercup.com, FAQs, official T&C · Last verified: 2026-09-26

## Who can enter
- **Working professionals, entrepreneurs and startups only.**
- **Students are strictly ineligible, whether full-time or part-time.** If any single member is found to be a student, the **entire team is disqualified**, even after shortlisting, selection or the finale itself.
- **Age 21+** at the time of registration.
- **JAPAC only.** Every member must be physically based in, and a legal resident of, a JAPAC country. Cross-country teams within JAPAC are allowed; anyone living outside JAPAC can't join.
- The organizer may verify **government ID, employment credentials and age at any point**. Non-compliance means immediate disqualification.

## Team rules
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | 2–4 members during the build phase. **No solo entries.** You can register solo and join a team later. |
| Single entry | One person, one team, one solution, one theme. Cross-team registration disqualifies every entry involved. |
| Roster changes | Allowed only while registration is open. **The T&C says Oct 4; the website says Oct 11.** See [timeline.md](timeline.md). |
| Team leader | Submits members' participation details when asked |
| Dropouts | Invite a replacement while the window is still open |
| Finale travel | Only **2 members** go to Singapore. Members 3 and 4 are barred from attending, even at their own expense. |
| Communication | Shortlisted members must respond on the official channels (Discord, Slack, Telegram or WhatsApp) and stay on the Hackathon Dashboard. Leaving these can disqualify the team. |
