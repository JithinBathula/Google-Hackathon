## Key clauses
**Registration & mechanics**
- Registering is free, and so are evaluation and mentoring. Nobody should ask you for payment.
- Register only through the microsite (Hack2skill).
- Registering means agreeing to receive Hack2skill emails and messages.
- The organizer can cancel, postpone or reschedule without refunding personal expenses.

**Tech stack & authenticity**
- Build primarily on the Google Cloud stack (Cloud Run, GCP, Firebase). Solutions built mainly on competing clouds are disqualified.
- Use only fresh code and **original assets** created during the official timeline.
- Deliverables: a live URL, a video under 3 minutes, a public GitHub repo, and a deck covering architecture and business case.

**Conduct & participation**
- Don't leave the online communication platform or the Hackathon Dashboard once the event starts; doing so can get the team disqualified.
- Shortlisted members must respond at checkpoints on Slack, Discord, Telegram or WhatsApp.
- A standard code of conduct and anti-harassment policy applies, including to the hacks themselves.
- No ads or business solicitation in submissions.

**Intellectual property: read this carefully**
- **Right of first refusal:** for **6 months** after the hackathon, the organizer (Hack2skill) has a right of first refusal on any *exclusive* license or acquisition of the submitted materials: software, code, docs, data and concepts. Before selling or exclusively licensing them to someone else, you must first offer the same terms to the organizer, who has 30 days to accept.
- **Publicity rights:** the organizer can publish and describe the submitted ideas anywhere, indefinitely, free of charge, in connection with the hackathon.
- **Marketing license:** the organizer and sponsors get a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive license to use marketing content derived from the entry.
- **Warranties:** you warrant that the submission is original, free of malware, and doesn't infringe third-party IP, privacy or publicity rights. You also indemnify the organizer against infringement claims.
- ⚠️ **For us:** check that our employer's IP and moonlighting policies allow this, since the hackathon IP terms may overlap with employment agreements. Don't use employer-confidential code, data or concepts.

**Media**
- Photos and video of participants can be published for **10 years, worldwide**.

**Eligibility enforcement**
- Participants must be 21+, working professionals, entrepreneurs or startups, **not students**, and **JAPAC residents**.
- The organizer can verify government ID and employment credentials at any stage.
- Entries that break third-party rights or the law, or contain objectionable content, can be disqualified.

**Data**
- Data security follows India's "IT Act 2008" (as the T&C words it).
