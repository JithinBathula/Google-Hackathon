Handover privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Handover is a prototype built for the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026. It helps a person who is leaving a job hand their knowledge over to a successor. It is a demonstration, run with a fictional company and synthetic data.

What we access

When you connect a Google account, Handover asks for read access to your Google Drive files and Google Calendar events, and for your email address. It reads the documents and events so that Gemini can extract decisions, unfinished work and open questions from them.

What we store

The text of the documents read, what was extracted from them, any answers you type, and your Google OAuth token. All of it is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Singapore (asia-southeast1) and processed by Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI. The OAuth token never leaves the server.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models. Handover's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can revoke Handover's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your stored data deleted, email jithin.bathula@gmail.com.
